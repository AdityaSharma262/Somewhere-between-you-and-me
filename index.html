A LITTLE NOTE

You must be wondering why I named our book…

Somewhere Between You and Me

Because, in so many ways, we come from different worlds.

We don't always think the same way. We don't always see things the same way. Even in the little things, our worlds can feel miles apart.

A place where we meet halfway, where we make the effort to understand each other, and where — despite all our differences — we keep choosing us.

Maybe that's what makes our story ours.

Not that we were ever the same, but that we always found our way back to the same page.
